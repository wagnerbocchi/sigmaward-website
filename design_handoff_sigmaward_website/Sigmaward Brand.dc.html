01 — Brand Essence

A lighter, modern alternative to Splunk, QRadar and Sentinel — Sigmaward turns Sigma detection rules into findings, promotes them into cases, and keeps watch over the entire security operation.

Personality
Trustworthy Vigilant Precise Technical Premium
The name

Sigma — the detection-rule standard at the product's core  +  ward — to guard, defend, keep vigil. A guardian driven by rules.

02 — The Logo

Sentinel Σ — the chosen mark

The Greek Sigma (Σ) is the ownable core — it ties directly to the product and reads as "summation": many signals resolved into one verdict, and as a forward chevron, a sentinel standing guard.

★ Selected
Concept 01 · Adopted

Sentinel Σ

A pure, geometric Σ that doubles as a forward chevron — the summation of signals and the sense of advancing, escalating, standing guard.

▹Ownable — tied to the Sigma core, never a stock security cliché.
▹Scalable — legible from a 16px favicon to a billboard.
▹Meaningful — many signals in, one verdict out; a guardian facing forward.
03 — The Mark

Anatomy & construction
01Summation

The two diagonals collapse many inputs into a single vertex — exactly what the engine does with Sigma rules: noise in, one finding out.

02Chevron / forward guard

Read as a chevron, the vertex points forward — vigilance, escalation, the promotion of a finding into a case. A sentinel facing outward.

03Geometry

Built on a fixed stroke weight with mitered joins and squared terminals — precise, architectural, never decorative. The vertex sits just right of center for optical balance.
